HaebotCase v1 (first version of the Haebot case) - 2026-11-18, Khurshidbek

Orientation: front = the narrow +X end (H B name, display, tray). Looking forward: A front-right, B front-left,
C back-right, D back-left.

Changes from v9
- Case 10 mm bigger on every side: 430 x 297 mm outside (was 410 x 277), still 510 mm tall (case top 560 mm
  above the floor). Wheels moved 10 mm further out with it (REF bodies).
- Name is now "H B" (Arial Bold, 36 mm tall, 0.8 mm deep), centred on the front at mid height (about 340-380 mm).
- Small vent holes: two dot grilles on the LOWER front and two on the LOWER back (motor area), 7 x 5 holes each.
- Power supply gate moved to the right side (-Y), centred front-to-back: 200 x 350 mm, 100-450 mm above the case
  bottom. Two vented screw-on covers sit flush on an inner frame (8 x countersunk M4 x 10 each).
- The bumper skirt is removed; the rounded wheel hoods are back, now on the long sides over the side wheels.
  Each hood sticks out 85 mm from the side wall (robot width over both hoods: 467 mm).
- Corners: every rounded corner is part of its quarter (not a separate print). The lines you see at the corners
  in Fusion are only where the curved surface meets the flat wall.

Body: 2 height sections (255 mm each) x 4 quarters. Needs 255 mm of Z. Largest footprint 235 x 169 mm.
Vertical seams: rounded lap tongue behind the neighbour wall, 0.3 mm clearance, M4 x 12 into captive nuts
(nut slides into the slot from the tongue end). The right-side seam only has wall below and above the gate.
Section joint: 10 joiner plates inside the wall, 2 x M4 x 12 each into hex recesses on the plate.
Top: shoulder unit runs side to side in 132 mm notches in both long walls; 4 lids with half-lap,
2 x countersunk M4 x 12 each into captive-nut lugs. Back lids have a 40 mm cable notch next to the shoulder unit:
cables run over the top basket to the back wall and down to the power PCB area.

Hardware (approx): 81 x M4 screws + 81 x M4 hex nuts
- seams 27 x M4 x 12 cap, joiner plates 20 x M4 x 12 cap, wheel hoods 8 x M4 x 12 cap,
  gate covers 16 x M4 x 10 countersunk, lids 8 x M4 x 12 countersunk, tray 2 x M4 x 25

Assembly order
1. Print coupon-seam B/D first and check the lap fit (adjust elephant-foot compensation if tight).
2. Join the 4 lower quarters, then the 4 upper quarters (nuts into the tongue slots first).
3. Stack upper on lower and fit the joiner plates.
4. Wheel hoods, gate covers, cables through the back-lid notch, lids, tray.
