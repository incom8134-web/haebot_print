## Assembly order

1. Build the 2020 frame (4 uprights, 3 rings at Z 50–70 / 262–282 / 493–513, extra rails at Z 120–140 front and right). Check diagonals for squareness.
2. Bolt the printed hook blocks and latch blocks to the rails (M5 T-nuts), and the tray mount to the front top rail. Install wheels, batteries, electronics and wiring.
3. Press heat-set inserts into every boss, key end wall, splice strip, latch block and fender lug.
4. Fit the 4 plinth segments onto the frame (2 bosses each). Fit the fenders: push the lugs through the plinth, then M3×12 + washer from inside.
5. Fit the lower corner posts, lower rear panel and lower left skin from inside (screws through the 2020 tabs into the bosses).
6. Fit the upper posts, upper rear and upper left skin. Each drops vertically onto the 10 mm tongue. No spline from the top.
7. Slide the 4 dovetail keys in horizontally from the long sides, tighten the M3×40, press on the magnetic caps.
8. Fit the brow (2 bosses) with the green light inserts.
9. Fit the shoulder segments: splice plates first (pins + M3), then screw each segment from below.
10. Bolt the tray through the front shoulder skirt into the tray mount (4 × M4).
11. Hang the doors and vent cassettes on their hooks, swing them in, tighten the captive M3, press in the slot caps.
12. To service: cassette or door first (no tools other than a hex key), then reverse the order. Shoulder removal needs the tray off first.

## Intentional changes from v1

- Front moved to the short +X end as requested (doors, brow, camera); vent cassette on the right long side; charcoal technical skin with light windows on the left long side.
- Body re-split from 8 × 255 mm quarters into ≤225 mm modules: 8 corner posts, 2 doors, 2 vent cassettes, 2 rear panels, 2 left skins, 4 plinth segments, 4 shoulder segments, brow, small parts.
- Load path moved to an internal 2020 aluminium frame; printed parts are now skins.
- Old joiner plates, M4 seam screws and countersunk lid screws removed; replaced by tongue + dovetail keys + insert bosses, all hidden.
- Old lids replaced by the green U-shoulder (same 132 mm channel and Z 537 notch, same top height Z 564).
- Power gate on the long side removed; front doors give 175 × 194 + 175 × 165 mm access, and the right vent cassettes are also removable.
- v1 dot-grille vents replaced by organised slot cassettes (~15–20× free area).
- Fenders: same shape and position; visible slotted screw holes filled, fixed by hidden lugs from inside the plinth.
- Tray: unchanged geometry and position; now bolted to a frame-mounted tray mount, so its load goes into the 2020 frame instead of the skin.
- Skin thickness 3 mm (v1 ≈ 4 mm), so internal volume is slightly larger.

## Assumptions / open points

- Wheels, cart and shoulder unit are not in the STEP (they were REF bodies in Fusion). Fender positions are unchanged, so wheel clearance is as in v1; please check against the real cart before cutting the frame.
- Insert hole Ø4.0 × 6 is a generic M3 value. Set `INS_D`/`INS_L` to your insert brand and confirm with the coupon.
- Frame-mount brackets (3 mm steel tabs) and corner brackets are listed in the BOM but not modelled.
- The native editable master is the Python parametric file (`cad/v3.py`). This environment cannot write Fusion .f3d; import the assembly STEP into Fusion to continue there.
- Shoulder segments are fixed (no top lids). Service access is through the doors, the vent cassettes and the open channel.
